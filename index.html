We keep watch over the heating in apartment buildings.

Kliimabot connects to the controllers of a building's heating system (boilers, district-heating substations and hot water) and watches them around the clock. When something goes wrong, the people responsible hear about it straight away.

Who it's for

Housing associations (korteriühistud), the maintenance companies that look after their buildings, and our own operators. We are piloting the service in Estonia now.

What we send by email

  • Fault alarms, for example when a boiler stops or hot water runs cold.
  • Sign-in links for the customer portal.
  • Monthly reports on how each building's heating ran.

Email goes only to registered users of a customer account. Users choose which alarms they get, and we send no marketing.

Customer portal

Customers sign in at app.kliimabot.com. Accounts are set up by us or by the customer's administrator.
